001 / Featured generative filmOriginal short film / May 2026

Dan Pradana / MXVDXN Studio

We found
something in...
[The Dead Mall]

At two in the morning, a gang of Jakarta scooter girls enters an abandoned mall. The night gets a lot stranger than anyone bargained for.

The thinking behind it

Freedom to make
strange things.

Format / Short filmTechnology / Seedance 2.0Platform / Runway

Generative AI gave Dan Pradana something conventional production rarely can: creative independence. Not just the freedom to make things faster, but the room to experiment with provocative, unusual and culturally specific stories that might never find their way through mainstream development.

One of those experiments became WE FOUND SOMETHING IN... [THE DEAD MALL]. Set in Jakarta, the short film follows a fictional gang of scooter-riding girls, loosely inspired by cabe-cabean, an Indonesian slang term associated with young women in street-motorcycle culture. It's a distinctly local starting point for a story that quickly goes somewhere unexpected.

At 2 a.m., the gang wanders into an abandoned shopping mall and discovers something that has no business being there. Instead of running, they stay. What follows is a chaotic mix of urban horror, absurd comedy and unapologetically Indonesian youth culture.

Created using Seedance 2.0 through Runway, the film became an independent breakout moment on X. Its reception showed that an unusual, culturally specific story could find an audience without following the conventions of mainstream production.

The most interesting stories aren't always the ones that get approved. Sometimes, they're the ones you finally have the freedom to make.

002 / Featured Gen AI narrativeIndependent short-form storytelling / Instagram

Dan Pradana / MXVDXN Studio

High School
No Saints.

Some lessons are never taught in class. At this Indonesian high school, reputation, loyalty and power matter just as much as grades.

The thinking behind it

The real drama
isn't on the syllabus.

Format / Short-form narrativeTechnology / Seedance 2.0Distribution / Instagram

With HIGH SCHOOL NO SAINTS, Dan Pradana takes the creative independence offered by generative AI into another familiar Indonesian setting: high school. Rather than telling a conventional coming-of-age story, he explores the unwritten rules that shape life behind the uniform.

The scene follows a fictional school world where feared girls command their own social hierarchy. Reputation travels fast, loyalty is tested and influence can matter more than what happens inside the classroom. The result is deliberately sharp, a little provocative and unmistakably rooted in local youth culture.

Made with Seedance 2.0, the project shows how Dan uses emerging filmmaking tools to turn a pointed cultural observation into short-form entertainment. It doesn't soften the idea to fit a traditional pitch. It gives the characters, tension and attitude room to play out on screen.

Its response on Instagram suggests that a distinctive, locally grounded premise can find an audience well beyond its immediate context. For Dan, the bigger takeaway is still creative freedom: the ability to make unconventional stories without waiting for the approval of conventional production.

Behind the uniforms is another curriculum entirely.

006 / About the person

Meet the
mind behind it.

Dan Pradana is a Jakarta-based copywriter and independent storyteller with a background in advertising agencies and a current role at a global payments technology company.

Outside his professional work, he creates original stories through MXVDXN Studio, from the action universe of SARI & PLOY to the supernatural absurdity of 9 NAILS and the gentler world of BETWEEN DAYS.

The creative habit has always been the same: notice something interesting, find the angle, write the story and keep experimenting until it feels alive. The tools just happen to be changing faster than ever.
