006 — The person

A little
about Dan.

Dan Pradana is a Jakarta-based copywriter and independent creative with a background in advertising agencies and a present-day role at a global payments technology company.

Outside work, his curiosity has taken him into generative media, not as a departure from writing, but as another way of doing it.

Through MXVDXN Studio, he develops original characters, serialized stories, and fictional worlds shaped by a love of cinema, music, pop culture, and the odd little things that stick in your head.

The medium keeps evolving. The impulse to make something interesting remains pretty much the same.
