BCA Finhacks: Making Blockchain Funny on Purpose
At BCA Finhacks 2019, the spotlight was on blockchain—a term that often sounds intimidating to the average person. My job was to make it click for a wider audience, without losing them in jargon.

So I wrote a comedic storyline that broke the topic down in a simple, engaging way. By using humor as the entry point, we turned a dense subject into something people could actually enjoy and remember. Tech education doesn’t always need to be serious, and this script proved it.
