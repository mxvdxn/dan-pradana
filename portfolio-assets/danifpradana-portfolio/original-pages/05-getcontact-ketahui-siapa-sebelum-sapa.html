Published 2021-04-29 · Advertising
GetContact Digital Video: When a Scam Call Hits Too Close to Home
For GetContact’s pitch, I wrote a digital video script based on a real scam attempt my parents went through. It started with a classic Indonesian setup—someone pretending I was in an accident. That personal moment turned into the core of the story, showing just how common and sneaky these scams can be.

I leaned into humor to keep the tone light while still delivering the message. The tagline #KetahuiSiapaSebelumSapa became the campaign’s punchline, reminding people to check who's calling before they pick up. The video ended up winning the pitch and helped launch GetContact into Indonesia’s digital scene with a strong, relatable first impression.
