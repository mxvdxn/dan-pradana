Indofood Maxicorn Launch: A Snack for the Bold, Built for the Game

For Maxicorn’s launch, I set out to connect the brand with a very specific mindset—young, energetic thrill-seekers I called “Hyperlifers.” They love pushing limits, but still play smart. That insight became the foundation of the narrative.

I leaned into PUBG Mobile culture, where high-stakes action meets strategy. The idea was to position Maxicorn as the perfect companion to that energy—bold, spicy, and made for players who live for the rush. It wasn’t just a snack ad. It was a lifestyle play, speaking directly to the adventurous edge of a new generation.
