Published 2020-09-03 · Advertising
Nutriclub Manifesto: Raising Resilience for Tomorrow
As a father, this project hit close to home. I wrote the manifesto for Nutriclub with the belief that today’s kids need more than just care—they need resilience to face a future full of unknowns. That idea shaped the tone and message of the video.

The narrative encourages parents to raise children who are not only healthy but also mentally strong and optimistic. It’s a message that blends my personal values with the brand’s mission, positioning Nutriclub as a supportive partner in every parent's journey.
