KlikDokter Manifesto: From Sick Care to Self-Care
For this video manifesto, my intention was to shift the audience mindset from reacting to illness toward valuing consistent self-care. Preventative health often sounds clinical and distant, so the challenge was to reframe it as something emotional, personal, and relevant to daily life. I grounded the message in a simple insight: when you feel healthy, you gain the freedom to focus on the people and moments that matter most.

The narrative was written with aspiration at its core. Health becomes the foundation that supports your ambitions, not just another task on a checklist. The line #JagaSehatmu served as a soft yet memorable call to action. It encourages people to protect their well-being as an expression of living fully, not out of fear or obligation.

Through this approach, KlikDokter is positioned as more than a medical platform. It becomes a reassuring presence in everyday life, a companion that guides users to care for themselves before problems arise.
