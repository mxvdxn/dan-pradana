Sweety Jingle: Turning Diaper Talk into a Toddler Earworm

For this campaign, I wrote my first-ever jingle—crafted to be fun for kids and catchy enough for parents to hum along. Inspired by the familiar melody of Tayo the Bus as the base, making it instantly recognizable and easy to sing.

The real challenge was packing product benefits into the lyrics without it sounding like a hard sell. I focused on simple, playful language that still highlights what makes Sweety Silver Pants a smart choice for parents. The result is a jingle that sticks, entertains, and educates—all in under a minute.
