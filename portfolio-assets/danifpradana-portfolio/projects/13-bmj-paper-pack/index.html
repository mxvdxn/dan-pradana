BMJ PAPER PACK

Published 2020-02-23 · Advertising · Copywriting · Writing
As a writer, I was tasked with creating a brand manifesto for BMJ, a leader in the cigarette industry known for their total solution approach to tobacco paper materials and packaging. The brief called for a focus on the philosophical value of the company, emphasizing the idea of "THINK OUTSIDE THE PAPER." This concept represents BMJ's belief that beyond a simple piece of paper, there are untold ideas waiting to be discovered. The manifesto was meant to highlight this philosophy and drive home the brand's commitment to customer experience and operational excellence.
TAGLINE & MANIFESTO

As a copywriter, I was responsible for creating the powerful tagline "THINK OUTSIDE THE PAPER" for BMJ, the world's leading partner in the cigarette industry. My role was to highlight the company's philosophical approach and bring to life the idea that beyond a blank sheet of paper lies a wealth of untapped potential and innovative ideas waiting to be discovered. The tagline was inspired by "think outside the box" but with a focus on the power of paper as the starting point for exploring uncharted territories of creativity and imagination.

PRINT AD

The manifestation of "THINK OUTSIDE THE PAPER" was not just limited to tagline, it also extended to the creation of a series of print ads for worldwide tobacco magazines. My role as the writer was to convey the company's philosophy through these ads, with "IMAGINATION" as the central theme running through the entire print series. The objective was to communicate the company's commitment to exploring uncharted territories of innovation and encouraging people to unleash their imagination. The focus was not on the product but rather on the core values of the company.
CORPORATE WEBSITE

I was also involved in creating a strong corporate image for BMJ's landing page. The main objective was to reflect the brand's "THINK OUTSIDE THE PAPER" philosophy through a corporate copywriting approach. I was responsible for writing the content that emphasizes BMJ's commitment to fostering imagination and innovation in their work. The landing page aimed to showcase the company's unique perspective and convey their values to the audience.
