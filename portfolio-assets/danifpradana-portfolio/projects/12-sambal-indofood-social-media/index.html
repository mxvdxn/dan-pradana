Advertising / Case study 12Sambal Indofood

Sambal Indofood Social

If the internet has a personality, a sambal brand can have one too.

Giving an everyday condiment a sharper, more culturally fluent voice in social feeds.

ClientSambal Indofood
DisciplineSocial media / Content series
My contributionSocial content ideas and copywriting
Behind the idea / 12
01 / The thinking

The product was already familiar

Sambal is part of everyday food culture in Indonesia. The challenge was not to introduce people to chilli sauce. It was to make a familiar household brand feel naturally present in the conversations younger audiences were having online.

02 / The thinking

Write for the feed, not the boardroom

The content takes cues from memes, local slang, playful exchanges, and pop-culture references. I developed a voice that could be quick, cheeky, and recognisably Indonesian. The brand behaves more like someone joining the conversation than a product interrupting it.

03 / The thinking

Consistency without repetition

A social voice has to stretch across many posts without losing its character. The work explores how to keep the humour fresh while making the brand feel at home in the daily scroll.
