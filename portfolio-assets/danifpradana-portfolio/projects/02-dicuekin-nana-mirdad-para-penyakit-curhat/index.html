Advertising / Case study 02KlikDokter

Dicuekin Nana Mirdad, Para Penyakit Curhat

What if sickness was the one feeling rejected?

A health campaign told from the least expected point of view: diseases complaining that nobody wants them around.

ClientKlikDokter
DisciplineCampaign film / Comedy
My contributionComedy concept and scriptwriting
Behind the idea / 02
01 / The thinking

Flip the usual health advertisement

People are used to hearing from doctors, parents, and concerned narrators about the importance of staying healthy. We wanted to make the same point without another stern lecture. So the script gives the microphone to the supposed villains.

02 / The thinking

The support group nobody asked for

In the film, personified illnesses gather in a therapy-style circle to discuss their shared frustration. Nana Mirdad and her family have been taking better care of themselves, and the diseases are suddenly having a very bad time. The comedy lives in how seriously these ridiculous characters take their own problems.

03 / The thinking

Humour with a purpose

The joke delivers the campaign message by inversion. Rather than showing people suffering from sickness, we imagine sickness struggling to get their attention. It gives #JagaSehatmu, or “Take care of your health,” a lighter, more memorable voice without turning the message into medical jargon.
