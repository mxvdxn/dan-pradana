KlikDokter Comedy Spot: A Group Therapy Session for Defeated Diseases
For the #JagaSehatmu campaign, I wrote a comedy spot that delivers a serious health message through humor. The idea was simple and memorable: imagine a group therapy circle where personified diseases gather to vent about their declining relevance. Their problem is Nana Mirdad and her family. They have become too healthy, and the diseases are losing their grip.

The script leaned into the awkward emotional honesty you normally see in support group sessions. I used that rhythm to create contrast. Instead of people sharing struggles, the diseases admit that their usual tactics are no longer working. By flipping the dynamic, the spot shows that consistent healthy habits can push sickness out of your daily routine.

The tone stays light and a little absurd, but every moment reinforces the message. Prevention does not need to feel heavy. It can be simple, empowering, and even entertaining. The comedy becomes the entry point, and the takeaway is clear: taking control of your health makes a real difference.
