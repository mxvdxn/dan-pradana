P&G #MaafIbudiHidupku: A Love Letter to Mom During Ramadhan
For this P&G campaign, I wrote a narrative that centers on the quiet strength of mothers and the gratitude we often forget to express. The goal was to create something deeply emotional but still relatable, especially during Ramadhan and the isolation of the pandemic.

I focused on stories where distance didn’t dull connection—where a mother’s love remained a guiding force, even from afar. The line #MaafIbudiHidupku became a gentle reminder to pause, reflect, and say the things that matter. It’s a tribute not just to moms, but to the emotional resilience families needed during that moment in time.
