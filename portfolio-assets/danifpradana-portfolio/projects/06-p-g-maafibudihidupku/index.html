Advertising / Case study 06P&G

#MaafIbuDiHidupku

Some of the most important words are the ones we keep putting off.

A Ramadan film about mothers, distance, and the gratitude that rarely makes it into everyday conversation.

ClientP&G
DisciplineRamadan campaign / Film
My contributionFilm concept and scriptwriting
Behind the idea / 06
01 / The thinking

The emotional reality of the moment

The campaign was developed around Ramadan during the pandemic, when families were finding new ways to stay close while physically apart. In Indonesia, the season is associated with reflection, reconnection, and asking forgiveness. That made the idea of an unspoken apology especially resonant.

02 / The thinking

Write for the things people struggle to say

I wanted the script to acknowledge a familiar truth: a mother's care is often constant, but our gratitude is not always expressed. Rather than relying on grand declarations, the narrative leans on small recognisable moments and the emotional weight of distance.

03 / The thinking

A hashtag with a personal voice

#MaafIbuDiHidupku can be understood as “Mom, I'm sorry for the times I've hurt you.” In the context of Ramadan, the line functions less like a slogan and more like an invitation to reconnect.
