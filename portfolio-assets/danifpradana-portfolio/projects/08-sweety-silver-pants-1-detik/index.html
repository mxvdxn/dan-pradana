Advertising / Case study 08Sweety Silver Pants

1 Detik

The smallest songs can be the hardest to get out of your head.

A playful product jingle designed for children, with a clear benefit message for the parents listening alongside them.

ClientSweety Silver Pants
DisciplineJingle / Film
My contributionJingle lyrics and copywriting
Behind the idea / 08
01 / The thinking

The two audiences in the room

Writing for a baby-care brand means speaking to parents without forgetting who else hears the message. A jingle has to be fun enough for children to enjoy and useful enough for adults to understand. Neither side should feel like an afterthought.

02 / The thinking

Make the benefit sing

This was my first jingle project. I leaned into the instantly familiar rhythm of children's entertainment, keeping the language simple, repetitive, and easy to follow. The trick was weaving in product information without turning the lyrics into a spoken brochure.

03 / The thinking

Designed to be remembered

A good jingle earns another listen through rhythm and playfulness. Here, the copy is not just what is said. It is also the timing, the repetition, and how effortlessly the message can be sung back.
