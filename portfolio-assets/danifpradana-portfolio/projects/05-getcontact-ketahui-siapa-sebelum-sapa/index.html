Advertising / Case study 05Getcontact

Ketahui Siapa Sebelum Sapa

The most convincing scam calls know exactly which nerve to hit.

A personal family scare became the starting point for a humorous film about checking who's calling.

ClientGetcontact
DisciplineLaunch pitch / Digital film
My contributionOriginal concept and digital film script
Behind the idea / 05
01 / The thinking

An uncomfortable true starting point

Someone once tried to scam my parents by claiming I had been in an accident. The story was designed to bypass common sense with panic. That experience stayed with me because the tactic was so recognisable: when family is involved, the instinct to act often arrives before the instinct to question.

02 / The thinking

Find the human story inside the product

For Getcontact's Indonesian launch pitch, I wrote a script grounded in that emotional reflex. Instead of explaining the technology feature by feature, the film dramatizes a situation that could happen to almost anyone. Humour helps turn an unsettling premise into something people can watch and remember.

03 / The thinking

A warning that works as a line

#KetahuiSiapaSebelumSapa translates to “Know who it is before saying hello.” The phrase folds a useful behaviour into everyday Indonesian speech. It gives the film its resolution and the brand a clear role in the story.
