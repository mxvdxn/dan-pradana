Maxicorn Product Launch

Made for people who never play it safe.

A bold product introduction shaped around the pace, language, and adrenaline of gaming culture.

ClientIndofood / Maxicorn
DisciplineProduct launch / Film
My contributionAudience concept and film writing
Behind the idea / 03
01 / The thinking

Meet the Hyperlifers

The audience insight was about a particular kind of energy. Young people who look for their next challenge, enjoy the thrill of taking risks, and know that good instincts matter as much as bold moves. We called them Hyperlifers.

02 / The thinking

Make the snack belong in their world

Instead of describing flavour in isolation, I built the launch narrative around the intensity of PUBG Mobile culture. Action, strategy, and a little competitive bravado gave Maxicorn a world to enter. The product's punchy personality could then feel native to the story, not pasted on at the end.

03 / The thinking

A launch with attitude

The writing keeps the tempo high and the language direct. It is about making an introduction that feels closer to entertainment than a standard product announcement.
