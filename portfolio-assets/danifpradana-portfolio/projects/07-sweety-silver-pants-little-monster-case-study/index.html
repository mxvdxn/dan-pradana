Sweety Silver Pants: When Diaper Rash Turns into Folklore Horror
For this campaign, we tapped into a well-known Indonesian belief—when babies get fussy, it’s because mythical creatures are disturbing them. We used that insight to create a playful twist: what if the “monsters” weren’t real, but just diaper rash waiting to happen?

I helped shape the concept and wrote the hashtag #GantiSebelumMenghantui, a reminder for parents to change diapers before the fussiness “haunts” the household. By mixing cultural folklore with a relatable parenting issue, we turned an everyday challenge into something memorable and uniquely local.
