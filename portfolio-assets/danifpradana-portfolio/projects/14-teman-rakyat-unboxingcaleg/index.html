Teman Rakyat - #UnboxingCaleg

Published 2020-02-25 · Advertising · Copywriting · Writing
WHO THE HELL ARE THESE CANDIDATES?

As a writer for "UnboxingCaleg, I was tasked with creating a narrative that would encourage young people to participate in the legislative election, despite the difficulties they faced in identifying qualified candidates. I leveraged this challenge as an opportunity to craft a powerful message that resonates with the target audience.

The goal was to educate and empower the youth to make informed decisions on election day, by highlighting the importance of their voices and the consequences of not using their rights to vote. Through the use of creative and impactful language, we aimed to inspire the public to get involved and help create a better future for all.


Our campaign was inspired by the concept of "unboxing" popularized in YouTube videos, where a product is revealed and its features and benefits are discussed. Similarly, we wanted to help the public unbox the unknown faces of legislative candidates and understand their visions and platforms, so that they could make informed choices in the upcoming election. The aim was to make the election process more accessible and engaging, especially for young people. All you have to do is visit www.temanrakyat.id and read our reviews of a certain legislative candidates. 

MEME VIDEO SERIES

To appeal to the millennial audience, the campaign created short, humorous Instagram videos that combined comedy and lighthearted jokes to make the information more accessible and appealing. The approach was a hit, with millennials responding positively to the entertaining and informative content.
INSTAGRAM POST TEASER

With the aim of encouraging more people to read the #UnboxingCaleg review, I also played a crucial role in creating Instagram posts that teased the review with witty and critical statements. By doing this, we were able to attract people's attention and encourage them to learn more about the legislative candidates.

The idea was to provide the public with information that was both engaging and meaningful, allowing them to make informed decisions during the upcoming election. Through these Instagram posts, I was able to bring attention to the important issue of lacking information about legislative candidates, and help educate the public by presenting information in an entertaining and accessible manner.

And the result is pretty impressive!
