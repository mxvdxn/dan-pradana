KlikDokter Digital Ads: Health Tips That Actually Stick
As part of the #JagaSehatmu campaign, I created a series of ad copies for platforms like GDN, Facebook, and Instagram. The challenge was making health advice feel less like a lecture and more like something you'd actually want to read mid-scroll.

Each ad focused on a small but relatable health concern—things people deal with every day. The goal was to deliver useful tips in a friendly tone, while nudging people toward a more preventive approach to health. It wasn’t just about awareness, but about making healthy choices feel simple and doable.
