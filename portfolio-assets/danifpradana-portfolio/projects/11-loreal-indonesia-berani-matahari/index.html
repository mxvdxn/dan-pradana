Published 2020-09-03 · Advertising
L'Oréal Berani Matahari: Sun Safety with a Wink
The brief was simple on paper but culturally tricky in execution: convince young Indonesians to take sun protection seriously in a context where sunscreen is often seen as optional, “extra,” or something your mom reminds you about. Instead of leaning into medical warnings or heavy education, I shaped a narrative built on humor, self-awareness, and everyday behaviors that felt instantly familiar.

My scripts used playful, conversational dialogue that reflected how Gen Z naturally talks about skincare. We highlighted common myths like “cloudy days mean no sunscreen” and turned them into light, funny moments that still delivered useful information. Every scene offered a practical takeaway that felt more like a friend’s advice than a PSA.

The tone stayed friendly, warm, and entertaining, which made the message approachable and worth watching from start to finish.
