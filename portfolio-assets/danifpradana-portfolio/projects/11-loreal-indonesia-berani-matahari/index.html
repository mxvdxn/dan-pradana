Advertising / Case study 11L'Oréal Indonesia

Berani Matahari

Sunscreen advice works better when it sounds like a friend.

A series of light, conversational films that meet Indonesian skincare habits with humour rather than a lecture.

ClientL'Oréal Indonesia
DisciplineDigital film series / Skincare
My contributionFilm series concepts and copywriting
Behind the idea / 11
01 / The thinking

A familiar barrier

For many young people, sun protection can feel like an extra step rather than an essential part of a routine. The brief called for a way to make the subject more approachable without flattening it into generic skincare advice.

02 / The thinking

Find the comedy in real habits

I wrote around the little rationalisations people make, including the assumption that a cloudy day is a reason to skip sunscreen. Familiar behaviours gave the scripts both their humour and their usefulness. The characters sound like people talking to one another, not experts reading instructions.

03 / The thinking

A little wit, a practical takeaway

Berani Matahari means “Brave enough for the sun.” The series brings confidence, warmth, and playful dialogue to a functional message. Each short film aims to entertain first, then leave the viewer with a reason to think differently about daily protection.
