Behind the idea / 01
01 / The thinking

The problem with talking about health

Most health messages begin with what might go wrong. The warning signs, the risks, the things people should stop doing. For a brand trying to make preventive care part of everyday life, that familiar language can create distance instead of connection.

02 / The thinking

A more human starting point

I approached the script from the other end of the conversation. Good health is not simply the absence of illness. It is what lets us show up for the people we love, pursue an ambition, and enjoy the small moments that make an ordinary day worthwhile. The manifesto places those possibilities at the centre of the story.

03 / The thinking

The line that holds it together

#JagaSehatmu means “Take care of your health.” It is deliberately simple, more like a reassuring reminder than an instruction from a doctor. The film gives that everyday phrase an emotional reason to matter.
