Advertising / Case study 10Nutriclub

Be Resilient!

We cannot prepare children for every future. We can help them face it.

A manifesto about raising resilient children in a world that rarely comes with guarantees.

ClientNutriclub
DisciplineBrand manifesto / Film
My contributionManifesto and film scriptwriting
Behind the idea / 10
01 / The thinking

The parenting question behind the brief

Every parent hopes to keep their child safe. But a future full of uncertainty also asks for something beyond protection: the confidence and resilience to respond when things do not go as planned.

02 / The thinking

From worry to possibility

Writing this manifesto felt personal to me as a father. I wanted the film to speak to parents with empathy, not anxiety. The narrative shifts attention from what the future might throw at children to the inner strength they can develop with encouragement and care.

03 / The thinking

A brand voice that feels supportive

The language is optimistic without promising an easy future. It positions Nutriclub as a companion to parents who want to nurture curiosity, confidence, and resilience, one ordinary day at a time.
