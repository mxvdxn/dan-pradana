02 / Behind the story

Freedom to
make strange things.

Generative AI gave Dan Pradana something that conventional production rarely can: creative independence. Not just the freedom to make things faster, but the room to experiment with unusual, provocative, and culturally specific stories that might never find their way through mainstream development.

One of those experiments became WE FOUND SOMETHING IN... [THE DEAD MALL]. Set in Jakarta, it follows a fictional gang of scooter-riding girls, loosely inspired by the local cabe-cabean stereotype. The Indonesian slang evokes a particular world of young women and street-motorbike culture, lending the film its cheeky, distinctly local attitude.

At 2 a.m., the gang enters an abandoned shopping mall. Inside, they stumble upon something that has no business being there. Instead of running away, they stay, and a reckless adventure turns into a collision of urban horror, absurd comedy, and total chaos.

Created with Seedance 2.0 through Runway, the film found an audience on X, generating 1.3 million impressions and 62,403 video views on the original post.

The most interesting stories are not always the ones that get approved. Sometimes, they are the ones you finally have the freedom to make.

For Dan, the appeal of generative AI lies in what it unlocks for the storyteller. It is a way to pursue an idea on its own terms, especially when that idea is too strange, too specific, or too much fun to explain in a conventional pitch.
